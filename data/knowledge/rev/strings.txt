Strings Utility in Binary Analysis

The strings command extracts printable ASCII or Unicode character sequences from binary executables.
Usage: strings -n 8 binary_filename (extract strings of at least 8 characters).
In reverse engineering, strings reveals:
- Hardcoded passwords, flags, or cryptographic keys.
- Embedded URLs, IP addresses, file paths, and debug messages.
- Imported/exported function names and library dependencies.
When binaries are obfuscated or packed (e.g., UPX), strings output may be hidden until unpacked.
