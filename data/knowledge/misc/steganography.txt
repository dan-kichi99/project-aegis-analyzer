Steganography and Hidden Image Data

Steganography is the practice of concealing secret information within non-secret files such as PNG or JPEG images.
Common techniques in CTF Misc challenges:
- Least Significant Bit (LSB) steganography: Hide payload in lowest bits of image pixel color channels (Red, Green, Blue, Alpha).
- Tool usage: zsteg (for PNG/BMP LSB analysis), steghide (for JPEG passphrase protected secrets), StegSolve (visual color channel inspection).
- Binwalk / Foremost: Check for appended hidden files or zip archives at the end of image files.
- LSB manipulation alters image pixels invisibly to human eyes while storing arbitrary binary data.
